# THE SOUND-TO-NOTATION SURVEY — v0, rough (2026-09-29)

*Opened at the composer's word (COMPOSITION_NOTES LG-126, RUNNING_LOG §546): the conceptual framework of `temporal_notation.md`
re-organized as an INSTRUMENT — a survey of evaluative questions and frames, the kind social science uses — not a set of rules.
He refines it in future sessions. The AI's wording throughout; the poles and leanings are lenses, never verdicts.*

---

## How to use it

- **Take it for any UNIT of sound** — one note that swells and changes colour · a gesture · a phrase · a passage. The scope is the
  unit's; the questions do not change.
- **Answer every item you can; skip the rest.** A range item is answered by a number 1 … 5 between its two poles; a pick by one
  of its words; a checklist by any number; a free item in a line.
- **Read the PROFILE (§P)** — what the pattern of answers leans toward: a layer, a carrier, a model, a test. A leaning is a lens to
  look through, not an instruction. You decide.
- **If the profile names something nothing on the page carries yet → a DEVICE SHEET** (`docs/PLANNING_METHOD.md`, line 1a). The
  survey comes BEFORE the sheet: the sheet asks how to draw it, the survey asks what it is.
- The framework behind each scale is in `docs/research/temporal_notation.md` (§6 the layers · §7 flow · §8 the working model ·
  §10 the decisions).

## The scales

### A · THE UNIT — what is the thing?

| # | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Grain.** What is this? | pick: one event · a gesture (a few fused events) · a phrase · a passage |
| A2 | **Edges.** How sharp are its beginning and its end? | 1 emergent ← → 5 sharp |
| A3 | **Learnability.** Could a player carry this from memory as one gesture? | 1 read as you go ← → 5 a learned lick |
| A4 | **Company.** Does its timing depend on other players? | 1 alone ← → 5 locked with others |
| A5 | **Length.** About how long is it? | seconds |

### B · THE INTENTION — what do I want from it? (T7)

| # | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Sound-image or energy.** Must it sound exactly so, or carry an energy with detail? | 1 exact image ← → 5 the energy, the spirit |
| B2 | **Welcome variation.** How much may a performance vary — in timing · in dynamic · in timbre? | three numbers, 1 none ← → 5 freely |
| B3 | **What would ruin it?** | pick any: casualness · imprecision · misreading · blandness · over-reading · (free) |
| B4 | **The rigor cue.** What on the page tells the player how seriously to take it? | free |
| B5 | **Portal.** Is the difficulty of reading it part of the music, or a cost? | 1 a cost ← → 5 part of the music |

### C · TIME — when, and how long (layers 1 · 2)

| # | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **The start.** How exactly must it land? | pick: on the cursor · at a cue, then free · inside a window · free |
| C2 | **The interior.** Is the rhythm inside the point, or only the envelope? | 1 the envelope ← → 5 the ratios exact |
| C3 | **Readable at speed?** At the scroll's speed, can the eye take the ratios from the spacing alone? | yes · no · unsure → measure (Q1) |
| C4 | **Pace.** Does the speed change inside it? | pick: no · in notches (a bracket, T6) · continuously (an accel — the GC chain) · both |
| C5 | **The line.** Does following the cursor help here, or interfere? | 1 helps ← → 5 interferes (→ the window, §8e) |
| C6 | **The length.** Is the duration an instruction, an outcome, or free? | pick: an instruction (a line) · an outcome (a decay, a breath) · free |
| C7 | **Synchrony.** Must anything inside it line up with another player? | pick: nothing · the start · a point inside · all of it |

### D · MOTION — what moves across it (layer 3)

| # | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **What moves.** Which parameters change continuously across the unit? | checklist: level · pitch · bow position · pressure · vibrato · speed · air / embouchure · colour · (free) |
| D2 | **Coupling.** Do they move as ONE gesture, or independently? | 1 one gesture (an intensity curve, H1) ← → 5 independent (their own lines; multitasking) |
| D3 | **Speed of change.** The slowest arc; the fastest wiggle | two numbers, seconds (→ V2: ≥ ~2 s a dial, faster a gesture) |
| D4 | **The shape.** What is the gesture's shape? | pick: a swell · a rise · a fall · a dip · a wave · a plateau · a step · (free) — the curve vocabulary, PLAN 2l |
| D5 | **Dial or lick.** Could the player follow a moving dial for this while playing? | 1 no, it is learned ← → 5 yes, a glance (→ V1 … V8) |
| D6 | **Paper alone.** Does a static line say it by itself? | yes · no (→ V7) |

### E · MOMENTS — the discrete detail (layer 4)

| # | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Switches.** Which changes happen AT a note — an accent, an articulation, a mute, a state reached? | checklist / free |
| E2 | **Density.** How much per-note detail does it want? | 1 sparse ← → 5 Ferneyhough-dense (T7: the density is the rigor message) |
| E3 | **Attachment.** Does each mark belong to a note (it rides the shape) or to a moment of clock time? | pick: the note · the clock · both |
| E4 | **Vocabulary.** Is there a glyph or a word for each yet? | yes · no (→ TODO item 8) |
| E5 | **Ornament or event.** Is a small fast figure decoration of a note, or an event of its own? | pick: an ornament (a grace group, A5) · an event |

### F · SIGN AND CARRIER — how does it reach the player? (Peirce, §7c)

| # | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **For each thing the player must know:** is it best DECODED (a symbol — a value, a word, a mark), SEEN (an icon — a curve, the spacing, a shape), or ENTRAINED (an index — the cursor, the GC, a fill)? | a short grid: thing → symbol · icon · index |
| F2 | **The model.** Would hearing / watching a model teach this better than any sign? | 1 the page suffices ← → 5 only a model teaches it (→ the rehearsal model, §7d) |
| F3 | **The now.** Does anything in it need to be shown AS IT HAPPENS? | yes → the viability test · no |
| F4 | **Count.** Would a count help learn it — and would it hurt performing it? | two answers, yes / no |

### G · THE PICTURE — the page against the sound (T3)

| # | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Picture of the sound.** Does the page look like what is heard — x is when, height is level, thickness is weight? | 1 no ← → 5 exactly |
| G2 | **Action or result.** Is any of it better written as what the HAND does than as the sound? | pick: none · some (which) · all |
| G3 | **The misreading test.** Could a casual reading of this page still produce the energy and the detail? | yes · no → what is missing? |
| G4 | **Dissonance.** Anywhere the spacing and the written values disagree (M2's rule)? | yes · no |
| G5 | **Agreement.** Does what the player sees at the scroll speed match what they will hear on the render? | yes · no |

### H · THE MODEL — closing the survey (§8d)

| # | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | **Which phrase model does the profile lean to?** | A1 default · A2 the learned cell · A3 the doubled carrier · A4 the gesture-glyph · A5 the grace burst · A6 a metric island |
| H2 | **The one new thing.** What must the page carry that nothing carries yet? | free → a device sheet |
| H3 | **The performer question.** What would I ask a player about this before deciding? | free (→ the consultation, §8a) |

## §P THE PROFILE — what the answers lean toward (lenses, not rules)

| If … | … the profile leans to |
|---|---|
| A1 = one event, D1 non-empty, D2 low | **one note, one curve** — an intensity curve (H1) with the words at its ends; the tube its animation |
| A3 high, C2 high, C5 high | **a learned cell** (A2) inside a **window** (§8e): the GC cues, the line steps back |
| C2 high, C3 = no, C5 low | **the doubled carrier** (A3): heads on their time AND values / brackets fitted — pay for the fit |
| C2 low, A1 = gesture, E5 = event | **a gesture-glyph** (A4): the envelope drawn, the interior free — the fast clusters |
| E5 = ornament | **a grace group** (A5) on its anchor |
| C7 = all of it, A4 high | **a metric island** (A6) — the count, locally, between the players who share it |
| C4 = notches | **a bracket** as a notch of speed (T6) — never a count |
| C4 = continuously | **the GC chain** (LG-122) — icon + index |
| D2 high, D1 ≥ 2 | **separate lines, a multitasking cost** — spend it deliberately, or couple the parameters compositionally (§7e) |
| D5 high, D3 slow ≥ ~2 s, D6 yes | **a local animation** — through V1 … V8 (§8c); one dial per player |
| D5 low, F2 high | **the rehearsal model**, not a sign (§7d) — the page carries the reminder |
| E2 high | **the density is the message** (T7) — do not thin it for the scroll speed; give the model instead |
| B1 low (an exact image) | rare for him (T7) — ask why; the model and the doubled carrier are the tools |
| G3 = no | **the page has failed** whatever its precision — find what is missing (B4: the rigor cue) |
| G2 = some | **action notation for those** (Lachenmann): a line for a continuous action, a word at a note for a discrete one |
| H2 non-empty | **a device sheet** — line 1a names the model, the rest draws it |

## §X A worked example — his case: *"a single note that crescendos and changes timbre"* (the AI's pass; illustrative)

- **A** · A1 one event · A2 edges 4 (a clear start, a shaped end) · A3 4 (one gesture) · A4 1 (alone) · A5 ~6 s
- **B** · B1 4 (the energy) · B2 timing 2 · dynamic 2 · timbre 3 · B3 blandness, casualness · B4 the curve's shape and the two
  colour words · B5 1
- **C** · C1 on the cursor · C2 1 (no interior rhythm) · C4 no · C5 1 (the line helps — it shows where on the arc) · C6 an
  instruction (the line) · C7 nothing
- **D** · D1 level · colour · D2 1 (ONE gesture — the colour rides the swell) · D3 6 s / none · D4 a swell · D5 4 (a dial works)
  · D6 yes
- **E** · E1 none · E2 1 · E4 the colour words: `ord` → `airy`? (no glyph yet) · E5 —
- **F** · level → icon (the curve) + index (the tube) · colour → symbol (the two words) + the same icon · F2 2 · F3 yes (the tube,
  already built) · F4 no / no
- **G** · G1 5 · G2 none · G3 yes · G4 — · G5 yes
- **H** · H1 A1 (it is not a phrase; the layers apply as they stand) · H2 nothing new — ONE curve named for two parameters (H1's
  question: does "intensity" cover colour?) · H3 "does one curve with two words at its ends read as one gesture to you?"

**The profile:** one note, one curve — the intensity curve with the colour words at its ends, the tube as its animation, nothing new
to build unless the colour must move on its OWN shape (then D2 rises and a second line is the cost).
